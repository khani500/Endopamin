Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 5, 2026

You can permanently delete your Endopamin account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to do it and what happens to your data.

How to delete your account from the app

  1. Open the Endopamin app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. Your account and data will be permanently removed.

If you can't access the app

If you're unable to sign in or access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us at info@endopamin.com from the email address associated with your account. We will process your request and permanently delete your account and associated data.

What data is deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently deleted:

  • Your account and profile information (name, email, age, height, weight, and fitness goals)
  • Your workout plans and workout history
  • Your nutrition plans and food logs
  • Your coach chat history
  • Your progress records and personal records
  • Any uploaded images (such as your profile photo)

Data retention

Account and personal data are deleted immediately upon confirmation. Limited records may be retained only where required by law (for example, transaction records for tax and accounting purposes). Anonymized or aggregated data that cannot identify you may be retained.

For more information about how we handle your data, please see our Privacy Policy.
